Why a website?

5 reasons why
your business needs one.

Whether you run a restaurant, a hotel, a craft business or a company, a website is no longer a luxury. It's an essential tool to attract, convince, and retain clients.

01

Your clients are already looking for you

Every day, people in Brazzaville type "restaurant Poto-Poto", "dental clinic Bacongo", "downtown hotel" into Google. Without a site, they land on your competitors. That's lost business you don't even know about.

02

Instagram doesn't belong to you

Your Facebook followers? You don't own them. Tomorrow the account could be blocked, the algorithm could hide your posts. A website is your property: your domain, your content, your clients.

03

A salesperson who never sleeps

An employee works 8h/day. Your site works 24/7, including holidays and while you're on vacation. Every night, it captures inquiries you would otherwise have lost.

04

You get judged in 3 seconds

A client torn between you and a competitor will look you up online. If they find nothing, or worse an abandoned Facebook page, they'll choose the other one. A professional website says: "this business is serious and trustworthy."

05

Costs less than a month's rent

Many think a website costs a fortune. Not true. A professional showcase site costs less than a month's commercial rent, is paid once, and lasts for years. No other marketing investment has such a lasting return.
