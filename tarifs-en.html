Frequently asked questions

We answer your last questions.

Is the price really fixed, no surprises? +

Yes. You receive a clear quote before we start. The amount doesn't change during the project, unless you request new features. No bad surprises at delivery.

Can I really pay in installments? +

Yes. For all packages, you pay 40% to start, and the balance is due at launch. 3-installment payment is available for the Professional and Premium packages.

How long does it take to build my site? +

Between 2 and 6 weeks depending on the package. The timeline also depends on how quickly you provide your content (photos, text, logo). We guide you through every step with a clear schedule.

I don't have a logo or photos. Is that a problem? +

No. We can help you create a simple logo and choose professional visuals suited to your business. These can be included in your quote on request.

Do I really own the site after delivery? +

Yes, 100%. Your site, your domain, your content. No dependency on l'Atelier KPM. If you want to switch providers in the future, you can. We hand over all access.

What if I need to update my site after delivery? +

You have two options: either you make the updates yourself (with our onboarding guide), or you subscribe to our monthly maintenance package. One-off interventions are also available on quote.
